Workbook notes & validation (per constituency)

Definitions

BJP share
BJP votes ÷ total votes polled (Voters Turnout → Total) in the village’s booths — the same basis as the workbooks’ Red / Amber / Yellow / Green bands.
Strongest rival
The non-BJP candidate (excluding NOTA) with the most votes in that village. Usually RLD or SP (the 2022 SP–RLD alliance), sometimes BSP.
Margin
BJP votes − strongest rival’s votes. Positive = BJP led. “Margin pts” is the same as a share of votes polled.
Turnout
Votes polled ÷ registered electors (Form-20). “Did not vote” = electors − votes polled: the pool for turnout drives.
To overtake
Where BJP trailed: the extra BJP voters needed to pass the rival (deficit + 1), or the number of rival voters who would need to switch (about half of that, because each switch moves the gap by two).
Booth band (from the workbooks)
Red <25% · Amber 25–50% · Yellow 50–75% · Green ≥75% BJP share.

Category & priority rules

Category (by margin pts)
BJP stronghold ≥ +20 · Lean BJP +5 to +20 · Battleground −5 to +5 · Lean rival −20 to −5 · Rival stronghold ≤ −20
High
Margin within ±10 pts, or BJP trails by up to 25 pts and the deficit is smaller than the number of people who did not vote.
Medium
BJP leads by 10–20 pts (lead to protect), or a safer village whose turnout is 3+ pts below its constituency’s average, or a trailing village outside the High rule but within 35 pts.
Low
Safe BJP villages with normal turnout (maintain), or deficits too large to close in one cycle (limit losses).
Verify
Not mapped to any booth yet — confirm the polling station on the ground first.

These rules are a starting point for planning, not a forecast. Local knowledge (caste/community arithmetic, candidate, local issues) should override them.

Data sources

The 12 constituency workbooks in raw_data/ (target-village sheets, match status and notes).
Booth results
2022 Form-20 files in form20 up/2022/ — every candidate’s votes and electors per booth. BJP votes were cross-checked against every workbook: all booths match exactly.
Map links
Approximate village-level coordinates from data-analysis/cleaned_data/booth_level/ where available; otherwise a map search by name. Verify on the ground.
